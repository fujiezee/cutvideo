# Edit plan: retro VHS home video

Goal: nostalgia. The young couple's Amsterdam as if found on an old family tape.
Format: 4:3 1440x1080, 30 fps, ~35 s, `fit: crop`, `focus: auto`.
Logline: a tape of the summer Thom and Celia were together, until it stops.

## Structure
| # | Section | Picture | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tape starts | Thom walking the streets and canals | ambience, score bed | PLAY ▶, SP, date stamp |
| 2 | Love letters | graffiti "Look Celia...", Thom, Celia, "...awesome in space", sunrise | their lines, tape-filtered | transcript captions in mono |
| 3 | The hand | "Celia I love your robot hand" graffiti, the kiss | lines | captions |
| 4 | It ends | "All right fine. I'm freaked out." | line | captions |
| 5 | Stop | VCR blue screen | silence | ■ STOP |
| 6 | Credit | black | - | attribution |

## Music / sound
Calm part of the score (from 44 s), ducked under dialogue. All audio band-limited
(`highpass 180 Hz, lowpass 4.5 kHz`) to sound like tape.

## Look
`vhs` grade (faded colour, chroma shift, softness, noise) + scan lines; short glitch (tracking)
on 4 cuts; date and SP stamps in a monospace face until the tape stops.
