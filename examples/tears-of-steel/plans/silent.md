# Edit plan: black-and-white silent film

Goal: retell the whole story as a 1920s silent short.
Format: 4:3 1440x1080, 24 fps, ~50 s, `fit: crop`, `focus: auto`.
Logline: a man chose space over the woman he loved; forty years later she broke the world.

## Structure
| # | Section | Picture | Text (intertitles) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | iris opens on the bridge | 钢铁之泪 / TEARS OF STEEL |
| 2 | The quarrel | Thom, Celia | 「听着，西莉亚，我们都得追随自己的热爱。」 |
| 3 | The hand | Celia shows the robot hand, Thom gives up | 「你就承认吧，你是被我的机械手吓到了！」 |
| 4 | Years pass | city, three battle shots | 四十年后…… |
| 5 | Reckoning | Celia, old Thom | 「你伤透了我的心。」 |
| 6 | End | iris closes | 剧终 / THE END + attribution |

## Sound
No dialogue (all clips muted). Quiet passage of the score from 92 s, band-limited
(250 Hz-3.2 kHz) like a gramophone.

## Look
`silent` grade (B&W, high contrast, grain, vignette), projector `flicker` on every shot,
motion at 1.15x, intertitles in a serif face with a double frame, iris in/out
(`circleopen` / `circleclose`).
