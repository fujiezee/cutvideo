# Edit plan: cyberpunk glitch

Goal: show the film's sci-fi, memory-hacking side as a high-energy neon piece.
Format: 16:9 1920x1080 full frame (`fit: crop`, `focus: auto`), 30 fps, ~32 s.
Logline: the scientists overwrite a robot's memory, and the system fights back.

## Music
Credits score from 0 s; `beat_sync` every 2 beats with `shift: auto`; the line in clip 2
ducks the music.

## Look
`cyberpunk` grade (cyan shadows, magenta highlights, high saturation and contrast);
`glitch`, `rgb_split` and `echo` on alternating shots; `pixelize` / `hlslice` transitions
between sections; neon text with glow.
